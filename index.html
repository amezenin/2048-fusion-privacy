Privacy Policy for 2048 Fusion

2048 Fusion is developed by Mezhenon Production.

2048 Fusion does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal or sensitive user data.

The game does not require user accounts and does not use advertising, analytics, location services, contacts, camera, microphone, or other sensitive device permissions.

Game settings and high scores are stored locally on the user's device and are not transmitted to Mezhenon Production or third parties.

If the application is removed, locally stored game data may also be removed by the operating system.

If the application's data-handling practices change in a future version, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.
